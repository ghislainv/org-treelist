This list is based on the tree species list provided by the [[https://www.bgci.org/resources/bgci-databases/globaltreesearch/][GlobalTreeSearch project]] coordinated by [[https://www.bgci.org/][BGCI]].

GlobalTreeSearch uses the tree definition agreed on by IUCN's Global Tree Specialist Group (GTSG): a woody plant with usually a single stem growing to a height of at least two metres, or if multi-stemmed, then at least one vertical stem five centimetres in diameter at breast height.

In New Caledonia, the list includes a total of 1365 tree species from 303 genus and 95 families.
